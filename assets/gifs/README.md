## Flower GIF credits

The two animated flower stickers in the Us scrapbook were sourced from Wikimedia Commons and resized for the page. Their reduced-motion PNG counterparts show a still, open bloom.

- `us-flower-red.gif` / `us-flower-red-still.png`: “Wikimania2023 Animated Sticker Flower 01,” illustrated by Adien Gunarta and Naila Rahmah for the Wikimedia Foundation. [Source and CC0 1.0 license](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wikimania2023_Animated_Sticker_Flower_01.gif).
- `us-flower-pink.gif` / `us-flower-pink-still.png`: “Wikimania2023 Animated Sticker Flower 02,” illustrated by Adien Gunarta and Naila Rahmah for the Wikimedia Foundation. [Source and CC0 1.0 license](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wikimania2023_Animated_Sticker_Flower_02.gif).
